# The Pitch

Business English that roasts you into confidence. A learning app for French-speaking professionals who understand English but freeze in meetings, coached by Alice, a sharp-tongued 40-something who is Helen's alter ego.

Made for Albatross Academy (Mazamet) by Helen Phillips, built with Claude.

## Where things are

- `content/content.json` — every word Alice and the app say: roasts, situations, rooms, lessons, rehearsal plans. Change the text between quotes; the app picks it up on the next update.
- `index.html` — the app. Right now this is the clickable prototype: welcome, voice check, home, the office-building Confidence Map, phrase bank, settings, a "Disagree politely" session and "Rehearse your real meeting".

## What comes next

1. Student logins with an invite code.
2. Alice's British AI voice, real speaking practice with the microphone, and AI-written meeting rehearsals.
3. A dry run with 2 or 3 students, then a 4-week test with 10 to 15.

The full plan lives in the project's plan document.
